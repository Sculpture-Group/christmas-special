Questions

Good to know before you book.

What is T-Shape 2?

T-Shape 2 is a noninvasive body contouring treatment used to improve the appearance of skin texture and cellulite and to support a more contoured look.

Is it invasive?

No. There are no incisions. Your provider will explain what to expect during and after your session.

How many sessions are recommended?

Plans are personalized. The Christmas Special includes three sessions, and your provider will advise on the right schedule for your goals.

When might I notice changes?

Timing differs from person to person. Your provider will review realistic expectations with you at your assessment.

Who may not be a suitable candidate?

Suitability depends on your health history and goals. Your provider will review this with you before treatment.

How do I book?
